# Black Ops 2 multiplayer (Free for All, Team Deathmatch)

This page is Black Ops 2 multiplayer only; zombies is [BO2-ZOMBIES.md](BO2-ZOMBIES.md).
Load assets from a locally owned BO2 installation:

```bash
IW4L_GAMES=/path/to/games IW4L_SOUND=off cargo run --profile play -p launcher -- map t6:mp_dockside
```

`make map ZONE=t6:mp_dockside` is the same. On Windows:

```powershell
$env:IW4L_GAMES = 'G:\Games'
$env:IW4L_SOUND = 'off'
$env:IW4L_ARTIFACTS_DIR = "$PWD\iw4l-artifacts"
.\scripts\play.ps1 map t6:mp_dockside
```

T6 uses its own installed common zone; no IW4 installation or retail executable
is required. Black Ops 2's compiled multiplayer scripts are not run: startup runs an
IW4L-generated `maps/mp/<zone>::main` (`game_t6/src/startup.rs`) on IW4L's VM, and
IW4L's FFA (`dm`) and TDM (`war`) rules (`t6_gametype.rs`) handle connection, spawn,
damage, death, score, archived attacker killcam, respawn and match limits/restart.
Use skips killcam; deaths without a camera retain the two-second delay. TDM balances
teams and blocks friendly damage. Other multiplayer modes refuse.

Spawns come from decoded map declarations, with ground/collision checks and
avoidance of other alive players. Movement and weapons are Black Ops 2's own
(`movement_t6`, `game_t6/src/fire.rs`). The initial loadout selects runnable
native bullet weapons, including a pistol secondary when available; unresolved
attachments remain refusals. Native class primary/secondary selection applies on
respawn. [Escape menus](MULTIPLAYER-UI.md) offer five saved classes, native item
pickers and live settings; full BO2 Pick-10 is not implemented.

Dockside verification covers movement/collision, jump, crouch, the 870 MCS
viewmodel, firing/reload, pistol switching, kill attribution, authored respawn,
HUD, death feed, score-limit intermission and automatic restart. Two clients
verified Hijacked/Raid FFA/TDM combat, team scores, respawns and match transitions.
Native fire/reload audio plays; optional missing aliases admit T6 with explicit
Degraded audio, required bank failures refuse. Player body animation is a
hand-picked table of 20 T6 clips (ledger: *Player body animation*). Frag
projectiles replicate and AN94 casings eject (scale and lighting unverified).
Broader equipment, perks/streaks, destructibles, other modes and maps remain incomplete.
The plan to replace these rules with Black Ops 2's own multiplayer scripts is in
[PROGRESS.md](PROGRESS.md) (Black Ops II — Multiplayer); its open rules are in
[fidelity/t6-mp.md](fidelity/t6-mp.md).

Capture with `screenshot t6-dockside-installed` after `admission admitted=true`
and `t6 player spawned` (output: `<artifacts>/screenshots/`; terminal stdin needs
`IW4L_CONSOLE_STDIN=1`). Keep the artifacts folder stable: `cache/wgsl_dxbc`
reuses validated shaders. On Windows with sound off (October 2026) a fresh
Dockside load took 271 s with a warm shader cache, 18.5 s in the same process.
