# T6 local runtime

Load assets from a locally owned BO2 installation:

```powershell
$env:IW4L_GAMES = 'G:\Games'
$env:IW4L_SOUND = 'off'
$env:IW4L_ARTIFACTS_DIR = "$PWD\iw4l-artifacts"
$env:IW4L_LOG = "$PWD\iw4l-artifacts\logs\windows-test.log"
.\target\debug\iw4l.exe map t6:mp_dockside
```

Build with `cargo build -p launcher`. T6 uses its own installed common zone;
no IW4 installation or retail executable is required; T6 bytecode is not executed.

T6 startup runs `maps/mp/<zone>::main` on IW4L's VM. Native FFA (`dm`) handles
connection, spawn, damage, death, score, two-second respawn, match limits and
intermission/restart. Other T6 modes refuse; other realms retain their startup.

Spawns come from decoded map declarations, with ground/collision checks and
avoidance of other alive players. The initial loadout selects runnable native
bullet weapons from prepared content, including a pistol secondary when
available. Native models/materials/animations need no IW4 weapon donor;
unresolved attachments remain refusals. Native class primary/secondary selection
applies on respawn; full BO2 Pick-10 Create-a-Class is not implemented.

Windows verification on Dockside: local alive spawn and camera, collision and
forward movement, jump, native 870 MCS viewmodel/hands/idle, clip decrement,
reload, Beretta 2023R switching, a normal shotgun kill against a dummy,
kill/score attribution and respawn at another authored spawn. Synthetic tests
also cover hitscan damage, duplicate kills, respawn, score limits and restart.
Crouch, HUD, death feed, score-limit intermission and automatic restart are verified.

Native fire/reload audio reaches the device with nonzero PCM and no observed underruns.
Optional missing aliases admit T6 with explicit Degraded audio; required bank failures refuse.
Basic native body animation binds 20 T6 clips through IW4L-owned movement/event rules.
Two real clients verified Hijacked movement, kills, scores, respawns and timed round restarts.
Melee visuals, equipment, perks/streaks, destructibles, other modes and broader audio/maps remain incomplete.
Capture with `screenshot t6-dockside-installed` after admission. Terminal stdin
requires `$env:IW4L_CONSOLE_STDIN = '1'`; output: `iw4l-artifacts/screenshots/`.

Dockside debug loads measured 577s before optimization, 396s filling the new
shader cache, and 271s on a fresh process with that cache (Windows, sound off).
Keep `IW4L_ARTIFACTS_DIR` stable: `cache/wgsl_dxbc` reuses validated shaders,
keyed by program contents, ABI and version. Preparation reuses geometry/IPAK
payloads and defers unused donor conversions.
Same-process `map t6:mp_dockside` loaded in 18.5s; reuse it for tests without rebuilding.
Audio-enabled fresh Dockside took 267s; switching to stock Hijacked took 210s.
Capture after `admission admitted=true` and `t6 player spawned`; screenshot
timeout is 180s and `wait world` timeout is 120s.
