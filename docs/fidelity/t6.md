# Black Ops 2 — fidelity ledger

Rule: Black Ops 2 matches run on Black Ops 2's own rules and data; no other
game's behaviour stands in. A rule that is not recovered is an `unknown!` in
`game_t6`, listed here with its id; `cargo xtask boundary` refuses an id that
is missing here.

Black Ops 2 ships its scripts as compiled bytecode. IW4L translates the compiled
modules (`gsc_t6`) and runs them for Classic zombies on every map but Origins.
Free for All, Team Deathmatch and Origins' Classic still run rules IW4L wrote for
Black Ops 2 on its data; each such rule is listed below until it is read from
the game itself. Some ids name a described deviation rather than an `unknown!`
in the code.

The compiled modules themselves are read: `fastfile_t6::scripts` takes them
out of a zone and `gsc_t6::Module` parses each one's header and tables
(includes, animation trees, code, strings, exports, imports). All 612 modules
in the eleven zombies zones of the Steam install (543 distinct names) parse,
each table ending where the next begins: 9 044 exported functions, 1.9 MB of
code (`maps/mp/zombies/_zm.gsc`: 168 functions; `_zm_perks.gsc`: 95;
`_zm_powerups.gsc`: 102).

Severity: **V** visible, **A** audible, **G** gameplay, **I** invisible.

## Open

### Zombies scripts and engine

- [ ] **G** Where Black Ops 2's own scripts (*Closed*: the bytecode, switch
      selector range `−0x7E8000..=0x7FFFFF`, and djb2 dvar hash resolution over
      all 3 004 registered dvars) still differ from the engine: the gametype's
      own zone is taken as `so_zclassic_<map>` whatever the gametype. A builtin
      not implemented fails as such (none on Nuketown, TranZit and Buried; Die
      Rise and Mob of the Dead are not measured).
- [ ] **G** Path nodes are read from the game world as the zone has them; the
      engine drops each to the floor at load (a −15,−15,0 to 15,15,30 sweep

      check; a node that fails becomes a bad node). *Needs:* that trace.

- [ ] **G** Actor movement (`natives::t6::movement`): `setgoalpos` and
      `setgoalnode` give an actor its goal (at spawn its own origin, radius
      4 and height 80; in goal is within both, as the engine tests it);
      `isingoal`. Each frame the engine's choice of animscript: no path is
      `combat` with an enemy, else `stop`; a path is `move`; a negotiation
      begin node (a `Begin` node naming an animscript, linked to the next
      point by a negotiation link) is
      `maps/mp/animscripts/traverse/<animscript>::main`, which the level
      loads for every such node as the engine does. The script left is
      notified `killanimscript` at once, before the next one starts.
      `traversemode` (`gravity`, `nogravity`, `noclip`, with the engine's
      error text) and `getnegotiationstartnode`. The node nearest a point
      is searched within 192 units, then 512.

      clip's root-motion distance along the path; the body steps up 18
      units, slides along walls and falls at 800 units/s², and turns 360°
      a second toward its motion (or what `orientmode` names); actors do
      not collide with each other. A traversal starts on its begin node (or
      within 16 units when the walk stalls) standing on it and facing the
      node's yaw; the clip's root motion carries it (through the world under
      `noclip`) with that facing held; a body the clip leaves inside the

- [ ] **G** Damage (`natives::t6::damage`): `G_Damage` as the engine
      dispatches it. Players and actors take nothing while `takedamage` is
      off, the damage is below 1 or they are dead (a player also when not
      `playing`, or hit on the riot shield). A hit names its weapon or takes
      the inflictor's, else the attacker's (a player's current weapon, an
      actor's own); melee keeps its damage, others are scaled by hit location:

      corpses and a freed corpse leaves its slot. A corpse takes the next
      place in the engine's ring of eight corpse entities, freeing the corpse
      that held it; its think, 300 ms after it formed, turns its contents to
      `0x4000000`. `t6.actors.corpse_entity` (**I**): the corpse keeps the
      actor's entity number and drawn body, where the engine moves the body
      to the ring's own entity number (54 to 61 in its layout, which IW4L's
      numbering, Modern Warfare 2's, does not keep free). A hurt actor records the same and
      runs its pain animscript when `allowpain` is set, `blockingpain` is not
      and the damage passes `minpaindamage`. Actors' clips are also every clip
      their state tables name (`zm_death`'s `ch_dazed_*`).

      `t6.damage.entities`) takes damage as the engine's own branch does:
      while its `takedamage` is set (a hit at the riot shield's location
      skips actors and such entities alike), the world for a missing
      inflictor or attacker, the inflictor's weapon for a hit with none, at
      least 1, its health less the damage (not a `trigger_lookat`'s), a
      `damage` notify with the eleven values (the struck bone's tag and the

      make (models, brush models, origins) have no pain or death handler of

      entities carry (damage triggers among them) are not run.
      `t6.damage.vehicles`: a vehicle that can take damage and has health

      nothing, as the engine's vehicle branch.

      `natives::t6::ragdoll`). `t6.actors.ragdoll_solver` (**V**): the
